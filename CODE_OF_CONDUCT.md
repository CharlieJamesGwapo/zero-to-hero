# Code of Conduct

ZERO → HERO is for people learning and teaching software development. Treat learners and contributors with patience and respect.

## Expected behavior

- Give feedback on the work, with specific examples and a path to improvement.
- Assume a question is sincere; explain terms when they are needed.
- Respect different experience levels, identities, and backgrounds.
- Credit other people's ideas and work.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when this standard is violated. Report concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub's available contact or reporting channel. Include links and context so the concern can be reviewed. Reports will be handled as discreetly as practical.
